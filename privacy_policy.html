1. Summary — what we collect at a glance

  • We do not ask for your name, email, address, phone number, or any other personal identifier.
  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which uses an advertising identifier.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to understand how the App is used and to diagnose crashes.
  • No audio, photos, contacts, location, or files on your device are read by the App.
  • For users identified as children, we switch AdMob to non-personalized, family-safe ads and apply additional data minimization — see §6.

2. Information we collect

The App collects the following information automatically:

a) Device and advertising identifiers

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID, or AAID) — a resettable identifier managed by your device.
  • Device model, OS version, language, country, and screen size — standard app diagnostics.

b) App activity and performance

  • In-game events (such as which level you opened or completed) in aggregated form.
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics when the App crashes or freezes.
  • Session timing (when the App was opened and closed).

This information is collected by third-party SDKs listed in §5. We never attach your name, email, or any personal identifier to it.

3. Information we do not collect

The App does not collect any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.
  • Financial information such as credit cards or bank details.
  • Health, fitness, or biometric data.
  • Messages, call logs, or contacts.
  • Photos, videos, or any files on your device.
  • Microphone audio or camera input.
  • Precise or approximate location (the App does not call location APIs; advertising networks may infer a coarse country from IP address — see §7).

4. How we use information

We use the information described in §2 solely for the following purposes:

  • Keep the App running. Diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and improve performance.
  • Understand usage patterns. See which levels are too hard, which features are used, and where players stop playing — so we can make the game better.
  • Show ads. Deliver advertising through Google AdMob to support the free version of the game. For children, ads are strictly non-personalized and family-safe (see §6 and §7).
  • Comply with the law. Respond to lawful requests from authorities when required.

We do not sell your information. We do not use it for behavioral profiling of children.

5. Third-party services

The App integrates the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to read.

Google AdMob (advertising)

  • Purpose: Shows ads inside the App.
  • Data accessed: Advertising ID, device model, country, approximate IP-derived region.
  • Policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Opt out of personalized ads: adssettings.google.com

Google Firebase Analytics

  • Purpose: Measures aggregate app usage so we can improve the game.
  • Data accessed: App events, session metadata, device type, OS version, country.
  • Policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Google Firebase Crashlytics

  • Purpose: Captures crash and performance reports so we can fix defects.
  • Data accessed: Stack traces, device model, OS version, breadcrumbs leading to a crash (never personal data).
  • Policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Google Play Services

  • Purpose: Standard Android app distribution, licensing, and update checks.
  • Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

6. Children's privacy (COPPA, GDPR-K, UK Children's Code)

Cannon Blast is designed to be family-friendly and is enrolled in Google Play's Designed for Families program. We take extra care to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the European Union General Data Protection Regulation protections for children (GDPR-K, Article 8), and the United Kingdom's Age Appropriate Design Code (Children's Code).

For users who are children (under 13 in the U.S., or under the applicable age in the user's jurisdiction):

  • We do not collect any personal information from children.
  • Advertising is configured as "child-directed treatment" in AdMob — only non-personalized, family-safe ads are served.
  • We do not enable behavioral advertising or interest-based profiling for children.
  • We minimize analytics data collection and never associate it with a persistent personal identifier.
  • Parents or legal guardians can contact us at dazzlecartsllc@gmail.com at any time to request information about what (if any) data has been collected and to request its deletion.

If you believe your child has provided us with personal information in violation of this policy, please contact us immediately at dazzlecartsllc@gmail.com and we will delete it from our records as quickly as possible.

7. Advertising and personalization

The free version of the App shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use the Advertising ID on your device and, for adult users who have not opted out of personalization, serve ads based on general interest signals associated with that ID.

  • Children receive non-personalized ads only.
  • Adults may opt out of personalized ads at any time via Google Ads Settings or by resetting the Advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID).
  • No ad network used by the App engages in cross-app behavioral tracking of children.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — ask what information is associated with your Advertising ID.
  • Deletion — ask us to delete any data we hold about you or your child.
  • Objection — object to how your data is processed.
  • Portability — ask for a copy of the information in a portable format.
  • Reset the Advertising ID — do this directly on your device.
  • Opt out of personalized ads — see §7.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at dazzlecartsllc@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

EU residents also have the right to lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority. California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) — the same contact process applies.

9. Data retention

  • Crash reports: retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days, then deleted.
  • Analytics events: retained in aggregated form; individual event-level data is deleted after 2 months by default (Firebase Analytics setting).
  • Advertising identifiers: we do not store Advertising IDs on our own servers. Retention by AdMob is governed by Google's own policies linked in §5.

10. Security

All data transmitted from the App to third-party services (AdMob, Firebase) is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. The services themselves run on Google's infrastructure, which applies industry-standard protection at rest. We do not operate our own servers that store user data.

No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but we take reasonable precautions to protect your information.

11. International data transfers

The third-party services used by the App (Google AdMob, Firebase) may transfer, store, and process data outside of your country of residence, including in the United States. Where required, Google maintains standard contractual clauses and other safeguards to protect international transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the Last updated date at the top of this page and, where the change is material, we will post a notice inside the App on next launch. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
